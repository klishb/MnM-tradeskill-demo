Cooking Tradeskill Progression Guide

Why Trivial Level Sorting Matters for Crafters

When a player visits a wiki tradeskill page, 90% of the time their primary question is: "My cooking skill is 45 — what should I craft next to gain skill points efficiently?"

Current Wiki Design (Category First) Groups recipes into Meat, Fish, Soups, etc. Forces players to open every sub-category, manually scan trivial levels, write them down on paper, and manually synthesize a leveling roadmap.
Proposed Redesign (Trivial Level First + Filters) Defaults to a single unified progression sorted by Trivial Level. Categories and ingredient sources remain fully accessible via instantaneous filters without breaking the leveling sequence.
